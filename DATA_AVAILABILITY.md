# Data availability and interpretation

This candidate contains derived numerical records and reconstructed computational workflows. It does **not** contain the third-party administrative boundary geometries or historical reference imagery. Public release is conditional on the author confirming the redistribution rights of the included research-derived material.

The 304-unit source table aggregates model outputs by harmonized administrative identifiers, but is not an independently verified legal designation map. The reference points cover selected detected candidate patches, not a statistically representative sample of unchanged pixels. Their ratio of confirmed detections is a selected patch-level precision estimate and must not be interpreted as omission error or an area-adjusted total.

GIS and GEE reruns require the correct Earth Engine assets and valid permissioned administrative data. Check `docs/PROVENANCE.md` and `docs/EXECUTION_STATUS_AND_STAGE6_RECONCILIATION.md` before reusing or citing computational outputs.
